Frequently Asked Questions

Where should robots.txt be placed?

It must be placed at the root of your domain, e.g. https://example.com/robots.txt, for search engines to find it.

Does robots.txt guarantee a page won't be indexed?

No, it only requests that crawlers not access certain paths — for guaranteed exclusion from search results, use a noindex meta tag instead.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The file is generated entirely client-side in your browser.
