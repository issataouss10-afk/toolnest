Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score?

Scores of 60-70 are considered easily understood by most adults; general web content often aims for 60+ for broad accessibility.

How is the score calculated?

It uses the Flesch Reading Ease formula, based on average sentence length and average syllables per word.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All analysis happens entirely client-side in your browser.
