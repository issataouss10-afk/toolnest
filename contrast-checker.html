Frequently Asked Questions

What contrast ratio do I need?

WCAG AA requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; AAA requires 7:1 and 4.5:1 respectively, for stronger accessibility.

What counts as "large text"?

Text at 18pt (24px) regular weight or 14pt (18.66px) bold or larger is considered large text under WCAG guidelines.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
