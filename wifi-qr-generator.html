Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to share a WiFi QR code?

The QR code encodes your network name and password in plain text, so treat it the same way you'd treat sharing your WiFi password directly — only share it with people you trust.

Does this work on all phones?

Most modern iPhone and Android camera apps can scan a WiFi QR code and prompt to join automatically; very old devices may need a separate QR scanner app.

Is my password sent to a server?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser using a client-side library.
