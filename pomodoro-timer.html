How to Use ToolNest Pomodoro Timer

  2. Start the timer: Click Start to begin your first focus session.
  3. Work until the alert: A break starts automatically after each focus session.
  4. Repeat the cycle: After 4 focus sessions, a longer break begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

It's a time management method that breaks work into focused intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) separated by short breaks, with a longer break after several cycles.

Does the timer keep running if I close the tab?

No, the timer only runs while the page stays open in your browser.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The timer runs entirely in your browser tab.
