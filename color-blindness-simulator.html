Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I check my designs for color blindness?

Color vision deficiency affects a meaningful share of people, and relying on color alone (like red/green status indicators) can make interfaces hard to use — simulating it helps catch these issues early.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The simulation happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — your image never leaves your device.
