Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between variance and standard deviation?

Variance measures how spread out the numbers are from the mean, in squared units; standard deviation is its square root, back in the original units, making it easier to interpret.

What if there are multiple modes?

If several values tie for the most frequent, all of them are shown as the mode; if every value appears once, there is no mode.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
