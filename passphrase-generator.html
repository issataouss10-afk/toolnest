Frequently Asked Questions

Is a passphrase as secure as a random password?

A passphrase with enough random words can have comparable or greater entropy than a shorter random-character password, while being much easier to remember and type.

How many words should I use?

Four to six random words is a common recommendation for a strong, memorable passphrase — more words for higher-security accounts.

Is my passphrase stored or sent anywhere?

No. Generation happens entirely in your browser tab and nothing is logged or transmitted.
