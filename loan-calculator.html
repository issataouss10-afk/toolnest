  1. Enter the loan amount: The total principal you're borrowing.

  4. Read the results: Monthly payment, total payment, and total interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?

It uses the standard amortization formula for a fixed-rate loan, based on the principal, monthly interest rate, and total number of payments.

Does this include fees or taxes?

No, this is a simplified estimate of principal and interest only — actual loans may include origination fees, insurance, or taxes not reflected here.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
