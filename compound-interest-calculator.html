Frequently Asked Questions

What does compounding frequency mean?

It's how often interest is calculated and added to the balance — more frequent compounding (like daily) results in slightly higher returns than less frequent (like annual) for the same rate.

Is this a guarantee of returns?

No, this is a mathematical projection assuming a constant rate — actual investment returns vary and aren't guaranteed.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
