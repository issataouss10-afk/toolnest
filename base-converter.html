Frequently Asked Questions

Why does binary use only 0 and 1?

Binary is base 2, so only two digits (0 and 1) are valid — each position represents a power of 2, just as decimal digits represent powers of 10.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversion happens entirely client-side in your browser.
