Frequently Asked Questions

What does the /24 mean?

It's the CIDR prefix length — the number of bits used for the network portion of the address, which determines the subnet mask and how many hosts fit in the subnet.

Why is the usable host count 2 less than the total?

The first address in a subnet is reserved as the network address and the last as the broadcast address, so they aren't usable for hosts.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
