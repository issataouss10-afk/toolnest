How to Use ToolNest Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Pick a unit system: MPG or Liters/100km.
  2. Enter the trip distance: How far you're driving.
  3. Enter your vehicle's efficiency and fuel price: Check your last fill-up or a nearby station.
  4. Read the estimate: Total cost, fuel needed, and cost per distance unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this estimate?

It's based on the average efficiency you enter — real-world fuel use varies with driving style, terrain, traffic, and vehicle load.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
