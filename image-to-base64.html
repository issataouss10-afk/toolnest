Frequently Asked Questions

When should I embed an image as Base64?

It's useful for small icons or images where you want to avoid an extra HTTP request, such as inline SVGs or tiny UI assets — large images are usually better served as regular files.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The encoding happens entirely in your browser using the FileReader API — your image never leaves your device.
