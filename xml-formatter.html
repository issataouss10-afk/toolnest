Frequently Asked Questions

Does this validate XML against a schema?

It checks that the XML is well-formed (valid syntax), but doesn't validate against a specific XSD or DTD schema.

Is my XML sent to a server?

No. Parsing and formatting happen entirely client-side in your browser.
