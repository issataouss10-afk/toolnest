How to Use ToolNest Neumorphism Generator

  1. Pick a background color: Neumorphism needs the element and background to match.
  2. Choose flat or pressed: Raised elements pop out; pressed elements look sunken in.
  3. Adjust distance, blur, and intensity: Fine-tune the softness of the effect.
  4. Copy the CSS: Click Copy and paste the rules onto your element.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the element need to match the background color?

Neumorphism creates the illusion of an element emerging from (or sinking into) the same surface, so the element and its background need to share the same base color for the effect to look right.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The preview and CSS are generated entirely client-side in your browser.
