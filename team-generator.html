Random Team Generator

Paste a list of names and split them into random, balanced teams instantly. 100% local — nothing leaves your browser.

⚡ Instant Shuffling

Names are randomly assigned to teams using your browser's secure random source.

⚖️ Balanced Groups

Teams are kept as evenly sized as possible, even with an uneven number of names.

🔁 Reshuffle Anytime

Click Shuffle again for a completely new random grouping.

🔒 100% Local

All processing happens in your browser's memory. Nothing is uploaded or logged.

How to Use ToolNest Random Team Generator

  1. Paste your names: One name per line.
  2. Set the number of teams: Choose how many groups you need.
  3. Shuffle: Click Shuffle Teams to see the random grouping.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if names don't divide evenly?

Extra names are distributed one at a time across teams, so team sizes never differ by more than one person.

Is my list of names sent to a server?

No. All shuffling happens entirely client-side in your browser.
