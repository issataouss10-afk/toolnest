Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between 16:9 and 9:16?

16:9 is the standard widescreen format for video and displays, while 9:16 is the vertical version used for phone screens and social media stories/reels.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
