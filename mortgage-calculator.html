Frequently Asked Questions

What is PITI?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance — the four components that typically make up a full monthly mortgage payment.

Does this include PMI?

No, private mortgage insurance (usually required with less than 20% down) isn't included in this estimate — check with a lender for your specific PMI cost.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
