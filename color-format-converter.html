Frequently Asked Questions

When would I need CMYK instead of RGB?

CMYK is used for print design (based on ink combinations), while RGB and HEX are used for screens and digital design — always check with your printer for exact CMYK values.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversion happens entirely client-side in your browser.
