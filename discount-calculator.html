Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't a 20% + 10% discount the same as 30% off?

Stacked discounts apply sequentially to the already-reduced price, so the combined effect is slightly less than simply adding the percentages together.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
