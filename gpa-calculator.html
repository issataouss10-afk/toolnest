Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade is converted to grade points (A=4.0, B=3.0, etc.), multiplied by that course's credit hours, then divided by the total credit hours across all courses.

Does this match my school's exact scale?

It uses the common US 4.0 scale, but some schools use plus/minus grading or weighted scales that may differ slightly — check with your institution for official calculations.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.
